Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Natural and Alkalized cocoa powder?

Natural cocoa powder is not treated with alkali, so it is lighter in colour and slightly acidic. Alkalized cocoa is treated to raise its pH, which gives a darker colour and a smoother, milder taste. That treatment is also why its allowed ash content is higher (12% max versus 10% max).

Natural Cocoa Powder: advantages

Fat 10–12% · Moisture 5% max · Ash 10% max

  • Authentic cocoa taste. Keeps the bright, slightly fruity character of the bean because it is not alkali-treated.
  • Lower ash content. Ash is capped at 10%, tighter than the Alkalized grade.
  • Natural light-brown colour. Suits products where a warm, light cocoa colour is wanted.
  • Works with baking soda. Its natural acidity activates baking soda for leavening in cakes and cookies.
  • Lower price. $5.0–$5.2 per Kg, the most economical of our two grades.

Ideal for: biscuits, baked goods, ice cream, beverages and fillings.

Alkalized Cocoa Powder: advantages
